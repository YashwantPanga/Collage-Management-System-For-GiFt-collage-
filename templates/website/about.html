About Us

A Journey of Excellence in Education

GIFT Autonomous, Bhubaneswar is the only engineering college in Odisha accredited with the A++ grade by NAAC. The college was conferred the prestigious "Gandhi Award" for Best Engineering College of Orissa State (2021) by the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE), New Delhi, and ranked 29th all-India and "Band Excellent" in the ATAL Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements (ARIIA) by the Ministry of Education, Govt. of India.

Founded in 2007 by the Balaram Panda Trust, GIFT is ISO 9001:2008 certified, approved by UGC and AICTE and accredited by NBA, IAO and NAAC (A++). It is recognised as a research centre by SIRO, Ministry of Science & Technology, and since 2012 has hosted national-level ISTE faculty development workshops on campus.
